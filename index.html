One move. Total chain reaction.

The classic chain reaction strategy game, rebuilt as an explosive 3D voxel war. Fill a tile to its limit and it bursts: your troops storm the neighbouring tiles, fight the defenders and take them over, and the next tile goes off, and the next.

  • 18 voxel armies, each with its own moves
  • 8 worlds, from frozen tundra to volcano
  • Up to 8 players: pass and play, or take on the AI
  • No ads, no account, plays offline
