Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

In short: Chain Reaction: Voxel Wars does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Chain Reaction: Voxel Wars ("the app") is made by Hanro van der Westhuizen, an independent developer in South Africa. For any question about this policy, email hanrovdwesthuizen@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting services. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things on your iPhone only: your settings (sound and aggressive mode), a game in progress so you can continue it, your Voxel Credits, and the races and worlds you have unlocked with them. This never leaves your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app which items your Apple Account owns so they can be unlocked and restored; that information is not sent to us. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply and do not share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
