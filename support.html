How to play

Players take turns adding a unit to one of their own tiles or an empty one. When a tile holds as many units as it has neighbours, it bursts: one unit charges into each neighbouring tile, fights, and captures it. Captured tiles can burst too, so one move can set off a chain reaction across the board. The last army standing wins.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock more races and worlds?

Aliens, Army and Zombies and two worlds are free. Play against the AI to earn Voxel Credits (more for harder and more opponents, and a bonus for winning) and spend them in the Unlocks screen: tap the credits counter at the top left of the menu. You can also unlock any race or world individually, or buy the All Races or All Biomes bundles, which include every one added in future updates.

I bought something. How do I get it back on a new device?

Open the Unlocks screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Items unlocked with Voxel Credits are stored on the device they were unlocked on.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my game saved?

Yes. A game in progress is saved after every move. Leave any time and choose Continue on the menu to carry on.

How do I turn the sound off?

Tap the gear at the top right of the menu and switch off Sound effects. The same screen has Aggressive mode, where neighbouring enemies taunt each other constantly.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. Only purchases and restoring purchases need a connection.
